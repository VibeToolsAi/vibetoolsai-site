Version 1.4 · Effective 21 November 2026 · Replaces version 1.3 of 14 November 2026 · Updated 22 November 2026: sign-up forms on this website (section 5) · Wersja polska

This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit this website, sign up for our free checklist or our emails, download or buy our digital products (printable planners, checklists, spreadsheets and prompt packs), contact us, or interact with our social media profiles. It is written to meet Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and also covers US (CAN-SPAM) and Canadian (CASL) email rules.

2. What you write in our planners never reaches us

Our products are downloadable files: PDFs you print and fill in by hand, and spreadsheets (.xlsx) you open in your own Excel, Google Sheets or similar software.

  • Everything you write in a checklist, planner or spreadsheet stays with you. We do not receive, store or see it. The files contain no tracking, no forms that send data and no connection to us.
  • Some of what you write may be sensitive (for example health information). Because we never receive it, we are not its controller. Keep your filled-in copies safe and share them only with people you trust.
  • Please never email us a filled-in page or a photo of one. If you need help, describe the problem in words or send a screenshot of an empty page. If you send filled-in content anyway, we will delete it and not keep it.
  • Our planners never ask for passwords, PINs or full account numbers, and we recommend that you never write them down.

4. Double opt-in, unsubscribing, withdrawing consent

  • Double opt-in. After you submit the form, you receive a confirmation email. You are added only after you click the confirmation link. If you do not confirm, we send nothing else, and the unconfirmed sign-up is removed from our list within 30 days after sign-up and permanently erased by our email provider within a further 30 days (section 8).
  • Unsubscribing. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link that works in one click. You can also reply “unsubscribe” or write to hello@getvibetoolsai.com. We act on it promptly; under CASL and CAN-SPAM, within 10 business days at the latest.
  • Withdrawing consent is free and possible at any time. It does not affect emails sent before, and you keep the checklist and anything you bought.

5. This website, our sign-up page and cookies

  • This website (getvibetoolsai.com) is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, uses no advertising or social media tracking and loads no fonts or images from other companies. GitHub records the technical data listed in section 3 in its server logs to deliver and secure the site.
  • Sign-up forms on our product pages send the email address you enter (and whether you ticked the separate marketing box) directly to MailerLite. The form on our site sets no cookies. MailerLite hosts the confirmation pages and may use cookies or similar technologies that are strictly necessary to display those pages, process the sign-up and protect it from spam and abuse. These do not require consent under EU rules. Details: MailerLite’s privacy policy (see “Tracking technologies”).
  • Visitor statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages and referring websites are popular. It sets no cookies, does not use local storage, does not create a profile of you and does not track you across websites. It records technical data about each page view (page address, referring page, browser type, country and timing) and shows us only aggregated statistics. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details: Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • We do not add advertising or social media tracking (no Google Analytics, no Meta or Pinterest pixel) to this website or the sign-up page. If we ever do, we will ask for your consent first with a cookie banner and update this policy.
  • Links to products include a label (for example utm_source=getvibetoolsai) that tells Gumroad or Stripe the visit came from this website. It contains no personal data.
  • Checkout pages are hosted by Stripe (buy.stripe.com) or Gumroad (and Etsy, if used), whose own cookie policies apply there. After a Stripe purchase you are taken to a Google Drive folder with your files; Google’s own cookie policy applies there.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Website server logs: kept by GitHub for as long as its own policy states for security purposes; we do not access or store them ourselves.
  • Unconfirmed sign-ups (confirmation link not clicked): removed from our list within 30 days after sign-up and permanently erased by our email provider within a further 30 days.
  • Checklist-only sign-ups (confirmed, no marketing consent): removed from our list within 30 days after confirmation and permanently erased by our email provider within a further 30 days.
  • Marketing subscribers: until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent. After that we keep a minimal record (email, dates of consent and withdrawal, wording version) to show we respected your choice and to make sure we don’t email you again, for no longer than the limitation period for claims (generally up to 6 years under Polish law).
  • Orders and delivery data: as long as needed to provide the product and support, then until claims become time-barred (generally up to 6 years; 3 years for claims related to business activity).
  • Tax and accounting records: as required by Polish tax law, generally 5 years from the end of the calendar year in which the tax payment deadline fell.
  • Your emails to us: up to 3 years after our last contact, unless needed longer for a claim or legal obligation.
  • Social media interactions: as long as the content stays on the platform; the platform’s own rules apply.

To use your rights, write to hello@getvibetoolsai.com. We reply within one month (extendable in the cases in Art. 12(3) GDPR). We may ask for information needed to confirm it is you, for example the email address you signed up with.
