Questions

Is this a will or a legal document?

No. Kept Kindly is an organizer. It helps you collect information and wishes in one place, but it does not replace a will, power of attorney or advice from a lawyer, accountant or financial adviser in your state or province.

Does it cover Canada as well as the US?

Yes. The planners are written for households in both countries and remind you to check deadlines with local authorities, because rules differ by state and province.

Can I fill it in on a computer?

Yes. Every planner comes with a spreadsheet (.xlsx) that opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers, alongside the printable PDF in US Letter and A4.

Do you see what I write?

No. The files have no tracking and no forms that send data. We recommend never writing down full passwords, PINs or account numbers.
